## Scope of corresponding source

Corresponding source includes modified code, dependency declarations and lockfiles, and scripts and instructions needed to build and run it. Binary distribution must include corresponding source or a free download link accessible without purchase or special permission. Source must match the exact distributed binary version. Linking only to the upstream homepage without supplying your modifications does not satisfy this requirement. Externally provided modified network services must also publish corresponding source for the actual running version. See license section 4 for how long download links must remain available.

Tasks, notes, personal data, passwords, tokens, deployment secrets, and private APK signing keys are outside source-sharing requirements. Deployment configuration may be supplied as templates for users to fill in.

## Patents, attribution, and breach

Patent grants cover only patents that contributors are entitled to grant and that their contributions necessarily implicate. Patent litigation against this software terminates the patent license. Modified versions must identify changes and dates and must not impersonate an official version.

A first breach is subject to a 30-day cure provision. Repeated breaches or failure to cure require separate reinstatement by the rights holder. Software is supplied as is; the maintainer promises no fixed maintenance, hosting, or response period.

## AI assistance and third-party attribution

Using ChatGPT, Codex, or their execution tools to assist development does not by itself require naming them as coauthors or copyright owners. OpenAI's [Terms of Use](https://openai.com/policies/terms-of-use/) assign output rights to the user as between that user and OpenAI, to the extent permitted by applicable law. This arrangement does not extend to third-party output or establish that all generated material has protectable copyright. Describe AI participation accurately when discussing development publicly; do not present AI-generated content as entirely human-written.

If third-party source, icons, fonts, or tools themselves are copied or distributed, retain copyrights, attribution, and notices required by their licenses. Participation by a generation tool does not replace these obligations. Describing the tools used and complying with third-party licenses are separate matters; project license section 8 does not alter third-party rights.
